## About Me

I specialize in building game worlds that feel intentional and immersive. My work combines environment design, terrain shaping, and light 3D asset creation to produce cohesive and visually engaging spaces.

I focus on structure, flow, and visual composition to ensure that every environment feels purposeful, whether it’s natural terrain, constructed areas, or hybrid scenes. I also enjoy creating small custom 3D assets to support and enhance the environments I build.
